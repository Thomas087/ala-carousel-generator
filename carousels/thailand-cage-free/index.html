4 — The supermarkets

9of 14

of Thailand's biggest supermarket chains have no cage-free policy at all. Only 5 have committed to stop selling battery-cage eggs.

Among those with zero policy: 7-Eleven, Big C, Villa Market and Foodland. Supermarkets decide which farms get built — their silence keeps millions of hens behind bars.
